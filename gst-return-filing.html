FAQs on GST Return Filing

Does quarterly filing mean I pay tax once a quarter?

No. If you make sales in the 1st or 2nd month of the quarter, the GST on them must be paid by the 25th of the next month (PMT-06). Late payment attracts 18% yearly interest.

What is IFF?

The Invoice Furnishing Facility lets quarterly filers upload B2B bills in months 1 and 2, so their buyers get credit every month. It is optional, at ₹499 per IFF filed.

What does GST return filing cost?

Nil return ₹200, up to 20 invoices ₹499 and above 20 invoices ₹999 per return. E-commerce sellers: nil ₹200, single platform ₹499, multiple platforms ₹999.

Do I need to file GST returns if I had no sales?

Yes. A nil return must still be filed, otherwise late fees apply. We file nil returns for ₹200.
