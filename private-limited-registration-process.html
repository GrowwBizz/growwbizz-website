Private Limited Company Registration: Documents and Process

The documents, rules and 3 simple steps to register a Private Limited company online in India.

By GrowwBizz · Updated 27 November 2026

Quick answer

To register a Private Limited company you need at least 2 directors (one resident in India), at least 2 shareholders (the same 2 people can be both), a registered office address, and a unique company name. It takes about 7 working days and is fully online. There is no minimum capital. GrowwBizz charges ₹899 plus govt fee and DSC, and you pay step by step, never 100% in advance.

Step-by-step process

  1. Name application (1 to 3 working days). You share two proposed names and your business activity. We file the name application with MCA. Only the govt name fee of ₹1,000 is due at this step.
  2. Documents and DSC (1 to 2 working days). Digital signatures (DSC) are issued for the directors, and we draft the MOA, AOA and incorporation forms for you to sign.
  3. Incorporation (2 to 3 working days). We file with MCA. You receive your Certificate of Incorporation, DIN for each director, and the company PAN and TAN.

Rules to know before you start

  • Directors: at least 2, and at least one must be an Indian resident (stayed in India for 120 days or more in the previous year).
  • Shareholders: at least 2. They can be the same people as the directors.
  • Capital: no minimum. Keeping authorised capital up to ₹1 lakh keeps govt fees low.
  • Name: it must be unique and must not look like an existing company or trademark. Give two options in case the first is rejected.
  • Office: any address in India with an electricity bill. If it is rented, you need the rent agreement and the owner's NOC. A home address can be used.
  • Name ends with "Private Limited" or "(OPC) Private Limited" for a one-person company.

After incorporation

Registration is only the start. After you receive the Certificate of Incorporation, two filings follow: ADT-1 (appointing the company's auditor) and INC-20A (declaring that the company has started business). We file both for ₹499 each plus govt fee. Every year the company also files its accounts and annual return (AOC-4 and MGT-7/7A). See LLP vs Private Limited if you are still choosing.

FAQs

How many directors do I need for a Private Limited company?

At least 2 directors, and at least one must be resident in India. The same 2 people can also be the 2 shareholders.

What documents are needed?

PAN and Aadhaar of each director and shareholder, address proof, a passport-size photograph, the office electricity bill, and a rent agreement with the owner's NOC if the office is rented.

How long does registration take?

About 7 working days: 1 to 3 days for name approval, 1 to 2 days for documents and DSC, and 2 to 3 days for incorporation.

Is there a minimum capital?

No. You can start with as little as ₹1,000. Keeping authorised capital up to ₹1 lakh keeps govt fees low.

Can I use my home as the registered office?

Yes, with a recent electricity bill. If the home is rented, a rent agreement and the owner's NOC are needed.

Do I pay everything in advance?

No. At the first step you pay only the govt name fee of ₹1,000. When incorporation starts, you pay our fee, the DSC and the remaining govt fees. There is no 100% advance.
