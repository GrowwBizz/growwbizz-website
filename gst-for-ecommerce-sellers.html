GST for Amazon, Flipkart and Meesho Sellers

What online sellers need to know about GST registration, marketplace TCS and monthly returns.

By GrowwBizz · Updated 24 November 2026

Quick answer

If you sell on Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, JioMart or similar platforms, you generally need a GST registration even if your sales are small. The platform collects a small tax amount (TCS) from your payouts and reports your sales to GST, so your own returns must match its reports. File GSTR-1 by the 11th and GSTR-3B by the 20th, monthly, even when you had no sales. GrowwBizz registers GST for ₹499 (pay after certificate) and files marketplace returns from ₹200.

Do online sellers need GST registration?

  • Most marketplace sellers do. The usual ₹40 lakh (goods) and ₹20 lakh (services) limits generally do not help sellers who sell through a platform that collects TCS.
  • Platforms ask for a GSTIN when you list products, so you cannot start selling without one.
  • Stock in more than one state (for example in different warehouses of a platform) may need registration in each of those states.
  • The government has been simplifying registration for small online sellers. The rules can change, so we check the latest position for you before we file.

Registration is free on the government portal. GrowwBizz charges ₹499 and you pay only after your GST certificate is delivered.

How marketplace GST works

  1. The platform collects TCS. It keeps a small percentage of your sales value and deposits it with the government in your name.
  2. The platform reports your sales. It files its own return (GSTR-8) showing your sales and the TCS collected.
  3. You file your returns. Your GSTR-1 must match what the platform reported. The TCS shows against your GSTIN and is adjusted when you pay your tax.

Mismatches are the most common problem: a sale reported by the platform but missing in your return, or returns and refunds not adjusted.

FAQs

Do I need GST to sell on Amazon or Flipkart?

Generally yes. Platforms ask for a GSTIN at the time of listing, and the usual turnover limits do not help most sellers who sell through a platform that collects TCS.

What is TCS for online sellers?

TCS is tax collected at source. The platform keeps a small percentage of your sales value from your payout, deposits it in your name and reports it, and you adjust it when you file your returns.

When do I file GST returns as an online seller?

GSTR-1 by the 11th and GSTR-3B by the 20th of the next month, and a Nil return is needed even in a month with no sales.

What if I sell on more than one platform?

Each platform sends its own report and TCS, so each is reconciled. GrowwBizz charges ₹499 per return for one platform and ₹999 for multiple platforms.

How much does GST registration cost?

The government charges nothing. GrowwBizz charges ₹499, paid only after your GST certificate is delivered.

Why do you need my reports by the 7th?

Platforms often update monthly data late. Reports downloaded by the 7th give time to reconcile them and file GSTR-1 before the 11th.
