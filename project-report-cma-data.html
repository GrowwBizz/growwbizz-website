What is Project Report & CMA?

Banks ask for a project report and CMA data to assess a loan. We prepare a clear report with projected financials, ratios and repayment schedule in the format banks expect. We prepare the report only; the loan application and sanction are between you and your bank.

Who needs it

  • New units applying for MSME or government scheme loans
