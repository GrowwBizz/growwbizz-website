LLP vs Private Limited: Which Is Better for You?

A plain comparison of the two most popular business structures in India, so you can pick the right one before you register.

By GrowwBizz · Updated 6 November 2026

Quick answer

Choose an LLP if you are 2 or more partners running a service business, do not plan to bring in investors, and want lower yearly compliance. Choose a Private Limited company if you plan to raise investment, issue shares to employees or co-founders, or want the strongest brand image with banks and large clients. Choose an LLP if

  • You are professionals or partners: consultants, agencies, architects, traders
  • You will grow from your own profits, not outside investment
  • You want fewer filings and no compulsory audit while you are small
  • You are converting a partnership firm and want limited liability

Choose a Private Limited if

  • You plan to raise money from angel investors or VCs
  • You want to give shares or ESOPs to co-founders and staff
  • You deal with large companies or government tenders that prefer companies
  • You want to apply for Startup India (DPIIT) recognition and investor-ready structure

FAQs

Which is cheaper to run, an LLP or a Private Limited?

An LLP is usually cheaper to run. It files Form 8 and Form 11 each year and needs an audit only above ₹40 lakh turnover or ₹25 lakh contribution. A company must get its accounts audited every year.

Can an LLP raise funding from investors?

LLPs cannot issue shares, so most investors prefer a Private Limited company. If you plan to raise investment, start as a Private Limited.

How many people do I need?

Both need at least 2 people. An LLP needs 2 designated partners; a Private Limited needs 2 directors and 2 shareholders, and the same 2 people can be both. At least one must be resident in India.

Is there a minimum capital?

No. Neither an LLP nor a Private Limited company needs a minimum capital. You can start small.
