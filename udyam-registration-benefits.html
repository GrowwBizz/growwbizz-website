Udyam Registration: Benefits and Documents (2026)

Why every small business should get a free Udyam (MSME) certificate, what you need, and how long it takes.

By GrowwBizz · Updated 13 November 2026

Quick answer

Udyam registration is the government's free, one-time registration for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. With a Udyam certificate, your buyers must pay you within 45 days, you get a 50% lower trademark fee, and you can use MSME benefits in government tenders. You need only Aadhaar, PAN, bank details and your business address. GrowwBizz files it for ₹499, with no govt fee.

Who counts as an MSME?

Classification uses both investment in plant, machinery or equipment, and annual turnover. These limits apply from 1 April 2025.

Both manufacturers and service providers can register: proprietors, partnership firms, LLPs and companies.

Benefits of Udyam registration

1. Protection against late payments

Under the MSMED Act, a buyer must pay a micro or small enterprise within the agreed time, and never later than 45 days. If they pay late, they owe compound interest at three times the RBI bank rate. You can also file a complaint on the government's MSME Samadhaan portal.

2. Buyers pay faster because of income tax rules

Under Section 43B(h) of the Income Tax Act, a business buyer can claim your bill as an expense only if it pays you within 45 days (15 days if there is no written agreement). That gives your customers a strong reason to clear your dues on time.

3. 50% lower trademark fee

With Udyam, the government trademark fee is ₹4,500 per class instead of ₹9,000 for companies and LLPs. GrowwBizz trademark filing is ₹799 + govt fee + DSC.

4. Government tenders

Government buyers reserve a share of their purchases for micro and small enterprises, and registered MSEs are usually exempt from paying earnest money deposit (EMD) in tenders.

5. Recognition and schemes

Udyam is the official proof that you are an MSME, which central and state MSME schemes and many large buyers ask for.

PAN cardOf the business, and of the proprietor, partners or directors
Mobile number and email IDOf each person, and of the firm, LLP or company
Bank account detailsAccount number and IFSC
Business address and main activityWhat you make or what service you provide
GSTINOnly if you are registered under GST

No documents need to be uploaded. Details are verified with Aadhaar OTP and PAN, and investment and turnover are fetched from your income tax and GST records.

Good to know

  • Free and lifetime: there is no government fee and no renewal.
  • One registration per business: add all your activities to the same Udyam number.
  • Category updates itself: if your turnover grows, your Micro, Small or Medium category is updated from your returns.
  • Beware of fake sites: the official portal is udyamregistration.gov.in. Never pay a "government fee" for Udyam.
