GrowwBizz App Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy covers the GrowwBizz Android app (package in.growwbizz.app), published by GrowwBizz, Madhya Pradesh, India. GrowwBizz provides business registration, GST, tax and compliance services. Our general website privacy policy also applies.

What the app collects

  • Name, mobile number, city and state that you enter, to identify your orders and reply to you.
  • Orders and bookings you place, including expert consultation slots and notes you write.
  • Documents and photos you choose to upload for an order, such as PAN, Aadhaar, photographs, address proof and business documents. The app only accesses files you pick yourself; it does not read your gallery or files otherwise.
  • Chat messages you send to our team in the app.
  • Payment confirmations you send (amount and the optional UPI reference). Payments are made in your own UPI app; the app never sees your bank, card or UPI PIN details.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, call logs, advertising ID or browsing activity, and it has no ads or third-party analytics.

How it is used

  • To provide the service you ordered, file your applications and returns, and deliver your certificates and documents.
  • To reply to your chats and send notifications about your messages and order status.
  • Due-date reminders you turn on are scheduled on your phone only.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Your details, orders, chats and documents are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and stored in GrowwBizz's own Google Workspace account (Google Sheets and Google Drive). Only our team members working on your service can access them. We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only where needed to complete your service, for example with government portals (MCA, GST, Income Tax, DPIIT, Udyam, DGFT, Trademark Registry) or a certifying authority issuing your DSC, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal and record-keeping requirements. Chats and uploaded documents that are no longer needed are deleted.

Deleting your data

Open the app, go to More > Delete my data. This clears your details from the phone and sends us a deletion request; we delete your profile, uploaded documents and chats within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep. You can also ask on WhatsApp 92036 01998 or by email at hello.growwbizz@gmail.com, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your phone.

Children

The app is meant for business owners and adults. It is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
