What is Private Limited?

A Private Limited Company is a separate legal entity owned by its shareholders and run by its directors. Owners' personal assets are protected, and it is the preferred structure for investors and banks.

Who needs it

  • Startups that plan to raise investment
  • Businesses that want to add partners as shareholders
  • Founders who want limited liability and a strong brand image
  • Businesses dealing with large clients or government tenders

What you get

  • Name approval from MCA
  • DSC and DIN for directors
  • MOA and AOA drafting
  • Certificate of Incorporation
  • Company PAN and TAN
  • INC-20A (commencement of business) filing: free when DSCs are bought from us, pay only Govt fees
  • ADT-1 (first auditor appointment) filing: free when DSCs are bought from us, pay only Govt fees

FAQs on Private Limited

How many people do I need?

At least 2 directors and 2 shareholders. The same 2 people can be both. At least one director must be resident in India.

How much capital is needed?

There is no minimum capital. You can start with as little as ₹1,000. Keeping authorised capital up to ₹1 lakh keeps government fees low.

Why does the cost change by state?

Each state charges its own stamp duty on the MOA and AOA. Use our cost calculator to see the exact amount for your state.

How long does Private Limited registration take?

About 7 working days, depending on MCA name approval and processing.

Can I register a company in any state?

Yes. We register companies across India online. Only the state stamp duty changes from state to state.
