MCA / ROC24 Oct 2026Due 31 Dec 2026

AOC-4 extended to 31 Dec 2026 and MGT-7 to 31 Jan 2027, no late fee

MCA has allowed companies to file financial statements (AOC-4 and its XBRL, CFS and NBFC versions) for FY 2025-26 up to 31 December 2026, and annual returns (MGT-7 and MGT-7A) up to 31 January 2027, without additional fees. Filings after these dates will attract the full additional fee counted from the original due date. The time limit for holding the AGM is not extended.

Who it affects: All private limited companies, OPCs and other companies filing annual returns for FY 2025-26

What to do: File AOC-4 by 31 December 2026 and MGT-7 or MGT-7A by 31 January 2027 to avoid late fees.

Official source: Ministry of Corporate Affairs · MCA General Circular No. 05/2026 dated 24 Oct 2026

GST08 Oct 2026

GST Council recommends lower penalties and easier compliance

At its 57th meeting the GST Council recommended removing arrest provisions under GST, raising the prosecution threshold from ₹1 crore to ₹5 crore and cutting the general penalty from ₹25,000 to ₹10,000. It also recommended simpler registration for small sellers on e-commerce platforms and faster refunds, and approved in principle an optional scheme for small consumer-facing businesses with turnover up to ₹5 crore.

Who it affects: All GST-registered businesses, small e-commerce sellers, exporters

What to do: These are recommendations. Each change applies only from the date the government notifies it.

Official source: GST Council · Press release, 57th GST Council meeting, 08 Oct 2026

Income Tax29 Sep 2026Due 21 Nov 2026

ITR due date for tax-audit cases extended to 21 November 2026

CBDT has extended the due date for filing income tax returns for AY 2026-27 by taxpayers whose accounts must be audited, from 31 October 2026 to 21 November 2026. The due date for the tax audit report was extended from 30 September 2026 to 21 October 2026.

Who it affects: Businesses and professionals covered by tax audit

What to do: File the ITR by 21 November 2026 to avoid late fees and interest.

Official source: Income Tax Department · CBDT Circular No. 07/2026 dated 28 Sep 2026
