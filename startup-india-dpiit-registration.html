What is Startup India (DPIIT)?

DPIIT recognition certifies your business as a startup under the Government of India's Startup India programme. We handle the DPIIT registration application, including the write-up on your innovation.

Who needs it

  • Private Limited companies, LLPs and registered partnership firms
  • Entities less than 10 years old from incorporation
  • Turnover below ₹100 crore in any year
  • Businesses working on an innovative product, process or service

What you get

  • Eligibility check
  • Startup India portal profile
  • Write-up on your innovation and business
  • DPIIT application filed
  • Follow-up till the recognition decision

FAQs on Startup India (DPIIT)

Is DPIIT recognition free from the government?

Yes, the government does not charge a fee for DPIIT recognition.

Do you help with funding?

No. Our service covers DPIIT recognition only.

Which DSC is needed?

An Organisation DSC (Class 3) issued in the name of the company or LLP. A director's or partner's individual DSC cannot be used.

Who is eligible for DPIIT recognition?

Private Limited companies, LLPs and registered partnership firms less than 10 years old, with turnover below ₹100 crore in every year, working on an innovative product, process or service.

I don't have a company yet. Can you help?

Yes. We can first register your Private Limited company or LLP, and then file for DPIIT recognition.
