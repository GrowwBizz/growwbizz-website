What is GST Registration?

GST registration gives your business a GSTIN. It is required once turnover crosses the threshold, for selling on e-commerce platforms, for inter-state supply of goods, and many other cases.

Who needs it

  • Businesses above ₹40 lakh turnover for goods (₹20 lakh for services)
  • Online sellers on Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho and others
  • Businesses supplying goods to other states
  • Anyone who wants to claim input tax credit

What you get

  • Choosing the right registration type
  • HSN and SAC codes for your products or services
  • Application filing and Aadhaar authentication
  • Reply to queries, if raised
  • GST certificate (REG-06)

FAQs on GST Registration

When do I pay?

Only after your GST certificate (REG-06) is issued and delivered to you.

How long does it take?

For startups and small businesses we deliver GST within 24 hours. If the GST officer raises a query, we reply for you and keep you updated on WhatsApp.

Is there a government fee for GST registration?

No, GST registration is free on the government portal.

Which documents do I need?

It depends on your business type. Open the Documents tab and pick Proprietor, Partnership, LLP or Company to see the exact list.

Can I register from home?

Yes. Your home can be your place of business. Give an electricity bill, property tax receipt or gas bill, plus a consent letter if the home is in a relative's name.

My premises is rented. What do I need?

The rent agreement and the owner's electricity bill, property tax receipt or gas bill, not older than 2 months.

Why does my company or LLP need a separate mobile and email?

GST needs a mobile number and email of the company or LLP itself, different from the directors' or partners'. All GST messages and OTPs go there.

Who must complete Aadhaar authentication?

The proprietor, or the partners or directors, complete Aadhaar OTP authentication on their own mobile. It takes a few minutes.

Do I need a DSC?

Not for a proprietor or partnership firm. Companies and LLPs sign with the director's or partner's DSC.

Can you do GST registration for any state?

Yes. GST registration is fully online, so we register businesses in every state and union territory of India.

What is the fee for GST registration?

Our professional fee is ₹499 and there is no government fee. You pay only after your GST certificate (REG-06) is delivered.
