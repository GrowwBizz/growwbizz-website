What is LLP?

An LLP combines the flexibility of a partnership with the limited liability of a company. Partners are not personally liable for the LLP's debts, and compliance is lighter than a company.

Who needs it

  • Two or more people starting a business together
  • Professionals such as consultants, architects and agencies
  • Existing partnership firms that want limited liability
  • Businesses that don't plan to raise equity investment

What you get

  • Name reservation (RUN-LLP)
  • DSC and DPIN for designated partners
  • Incorporation filing (FiLLiP)
  • Certificate of Incorporation
  • LLP PAN and TAN
  • LLP Agreement drafting
  • LLP Agreement filing (Form 3): free when DSCs are bought from us, pay only Govt fees and stamp duty

FAQs on LLP

How many partners do I need?

At least 2 designated partners, and at least one must be resident in India.

What is Form 3?

Form 3 files your LLP Agreement with MCA. It must be filed within 30 days of incorporation, or a late fee of ₹100 per day applies.

LLP or Private Limited?

Choose an LLP for lower compliance between partners. Choose a Private Limited if you want to raise investment. See our comparison on the home page.

How long does LLP registration take?

About 7 working days for incorporation. The LLP Agreement (Form 3) is filed within 30 days after that.

Is there a minimum capital for an LLP?

No. There is no minimum contribution. The ₹900 govt fee covers a contribution up to ₹1 lakh.
