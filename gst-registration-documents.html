GST Registration Documents List (2026)

The exact documents you need for GST registration in India, by business type, and the mistakes that delay approval.

By GrowwBizz · Updated 3 November 2026

Quick answer

For GST registration you need the PAN and Aadhaar of the owner (or of every partner or director), a photo, a mobile number and email, and proof of your business address such as an electricity bill, property tax receipt or gas bill. A rent agreement is needed if the place is rented. Firms, LLPs and companies also need their own PAN and registration documents. There is no government fee.

Common mistakes that delay GST registration

  • Old electricity bill. The bill must be recent, not older than 2 months, and show the full address.
  • Aadhaar not linked to mobile. Each proprietor, partner or director completes Aadhaar OTP authentication, so the Aadhaar must be linked to a working mobile number.
  • Same mobile and email for the company and its directors. A company or LLP needs its own mobile number and email, different from the directors' or partners'.
  • Home in a relative's name without a consent letter. If the premises is owned by a relative, add a consent letter from the owner. We share the format.
  • Wrong business category or HSN/SAC codes. These decide your returns and tax rate later, so choose them carefully.

FAQs

Is there a government fee for GST registration?

No. GST registration is free on the government portal. GrowwBizz charges ₹499, paid only after your GST certificate is delivered.

Can I register for GST from home?

Yes. Your home can be your place of business. Give an electricity bill, property tax receipt or gas bill, plus a consent letter if the home is in a relative's name.

My premises is rented. What do I need?

The rent agreement and the owner's electricity bill, property tax receipt or gas bill, not older than 2 months.

Do I need a DSC for GST registration?

Not for a proprietor or partnership firm. Companies and LLPs sign with the director's or partner's DSC.
