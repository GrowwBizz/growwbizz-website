What is CMA Data and a Project Report?

What banks mean by CMA data and a project report, what goes into them, and how to get yours ready in 24 hours.

By GrowwBizz · Updated 17 November 2026

Quick answer

CMA data (Credit Monitoring Arrangement data) is a set of standard statements a bank asks for before it considers a business loan or working capital limit. It shows your past results, current position and projected sales, profit, balance sheet and cash flow. A project report is the written story behind those numbers: your business, project cost, how it will earn, and how it will repay. GrowwBizz prepares both, in bank format, within 24 hours, from ₹999. We prepare the reports only; the application and sanction are between you and your bank.

What is in CMA data?

Banks use a standard set of forms. The exact layout can vary by bank, but it usually covers:

  • Existing and proposed limits: what you have today and what you are asking for
  • Operating statement: sales, costs and profit, for past years and projected years
  • Balance sheet analysis: assets, liabilities, net worth, in a bank-friendly grouping
  • Current assets and current liabilities: stock, debtors, creditors and working capital
  • Fund flow: where money comes from and where it goes
  • Key ratios: current ratio, debt-equity ratio and DSCR (debt service coverage ratio)

What is in a project report?

  • Business profile: promoters, activity, location, market
  • Project cost and how it is funded: your own money and the bank's part
  • Projected sales, expenses and profit for the coming years
  • Projected balance sheet and cash flow
  • Repayment schedule and DSCR, which tell the bank how comfortably you can repay

A clear, realistic report helps the bank understand your business quickly. Unrealistic numbers are one of the most common reasons reports get questioned, so we help you estimate sales and expenses sensibly.

When do you need them?

  • Applying for a term loan to buy machinery or set up a unit
  • Applying for a working capital limit such as cash credit or overdraft
  • Applying under government schemes that need a project report
  • Renewing or enhancing an existing limit, when the bank asks for fresh projections

FAQs

What does CMA stand for?

CMA stands for Credit Monitoring Arrangement. CMA data is a set of standard financial statements, past and projected, that banks use to assess a business loan or working capital limit.

What is the difference between a project report and CMA data?

A project report is a written plan with your business profile, project cost and projected earnings. CMA data is the set of standard bank statements with figures and ratios. Banks usually want both.
