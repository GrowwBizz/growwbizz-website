DPIIT Recognition: Who Is Eligible? (2026)

The simple checklist for DPIIT startup recognition under Startup India, and what you need to apply.

By GrowwBizz · Updated 20 November 2026

Quick answer

Your business can get DPIIT recognition under Startup India if it is a Private Limited company, LLP or registered partnership firm, is less than 10 years old from incorporation, has turnover of ₹200 crore or less in every financial year, and is working on an innovative or scalable product, process or service. The government fee is nil. GrowwBizz files it for ₹999 plus an organisation DSC. We do DPIIT recognition only.

DPIIT eligibility checklist

  • Type of entity: a Private Limited company, an LLP or a registered partnership firm. A sole proprietorship cannot be recognised, so register a company or LLP first.
  • Age: not more than 10 years from the date of incorporation or registration.
  • Turnover: not more than ₹200 crore in any financial year since incorporation. In the earlier rules this limit was ₹100 crore; the government raised it in February 2026.
  • Innovation: working towards innovation, development or improvement of products, processes or services, or running a scalable business model with high potential for jobs or wealth creation.

Deep-tech startups get a longer window and a higher turnover limit under the same notification. Co-operative societies can now also apply, but most small businesses will register as a Private Limited company or LLP.

What "innovative" means in practice

You do not need a patent. The application asks you to explain, in plain words, what problem you solve and what is new or better about how you do it. A new product, a better process, a new use of technology, or a business model that can grow without growing costs at the same rate can all qualify. A normal shop or a copy of an existing service, with nothing new, is usually not enough. This write-up is the part most applications are questioned on, and we help you draft it.

Why get DPIIT recognition?

  • Official recognition as a startup under the Government of India's Startup India programme
  • Eligibility to apply separately for the Section 80-IAC income tax exemption, which needs its own approval and conditions
  • Access to the other benefits listed on the Startup India portal
  • Added credibility with customers, partners and officials

Recognition does not by itself give any tax benefit or funding. The government can also revoke it if the details given were wrong or the conditions are not followed, so apply with accurate information.

FAQs

Who is eligible for DPIIT recognition?

A Private Limited company, LLP or registered partnership firm that is not more than 10 years old from incorporation, has turnover of ₹200 crore or less in every financial year, and works on an innovative product, process or service or a scalable business model.

Can a proprietorship get DPIIT recognition?

No. Only registered entities such as a Private Limited company, LLP or partnership firm can apply. A proprietor can first register a company or LLP.

Is there a government fee?

No. The government does not charge a fee for DPIIT recognition. GrowwBizz charges ₹999, paid in steps, plus an organisation DSC if you do not have one.

Do I need a patent to be recognised?

No. You need to explain clearly what is new or better about your product, process or service. We help you write it.

Which DSC is needed?

An organisation DSC (Class 3) in the name of the company or LLP. A director's or partner's individual DSC cannot be used. We can issue it for ₹2,000.

No. Our service covers DPIIT recognition only.
