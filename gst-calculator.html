How GST is calculated

Adding GST: GST = price × rate ÷ 100. Total = price + GST.

Removing GST: price before GST = inclusive price ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100). GST = inclusive price − price before GST.

Split: within your state, half is CGST and half is SGST. For supply to another state, the whole amount is IGST.

FAQs

How do I add GST to a price?

Multiply the price by the GST rate. For ₹1,000 at 18%, GST is ₹180 and the total is ₹1,180.

How do I remove GST from an inclusive price?

Divide the price by (1 + rate). For ₹1,180 including 18% GST, the price before GST is 1,180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000 and GST is ₹180.

When is it CGST + SGST and when is it IGST?

Within the same state, GST is split equally into CGST and SGST. When goods or services go to another state, the full amount is IGST.
