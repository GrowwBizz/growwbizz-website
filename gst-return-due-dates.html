GST Return Due Dates: GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B (2026)

When to file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B every month or quarter, and what happens if you file late.

By GrowwBizz · Updated 10 November 2026

Quick answer

If you file monthly, GSTR-1 is due on the 11th and GSTR-3B on the 20th of the next month. If you file quarterly (QRMP scheme, turnover up to ₹5 crore), GSTR-1 is due on the 13th of the month after the quarter, and GSTR-3B on the 22nd or 24th depending on your state. You must file even when you had no sales.

Monthly filers

ReturnWhat it isDue date
GSTR-1Details of your sales invoices11th of the next month

Example: for October sales, GSTR-1 is due on 11 November and GSTR-3B on 20 November.

Quarterly filers (QRMP scheme)

Businesses with aggregate turnover up to ₹5 crore can choose to file returns every quarter but pay tax every month.

Late fee and interest

  • Late fee: ₹50 per day for GSTR-1 or GSTR-3B (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST), and ₹20 per day for a Nil return.
  • Maximum late fee per return: ₹500 for a Nil return; ₹2,000 if turnover is up to ₹1.5 crore; ₹5,000 for ₹1.5 to 5 crore; ₹10,000 above ₹5 crore.
  • Interest: 18% a year on tax paid late, counted from the day after the due date.
  • Blocked filing: if GSTR-3B is pending, you cannot file the next GSTR-1, and your buyers may not get input tax credit.
  • 3-year limit: a return cannot be filed once 3 years have passed from its due date, so do not leave old returns pending.

FAQs

What is the due date for GSTR-1?

The 11th of the next month for monthly filers, and the 13th of the month after the quarter for quarterly (QRMP) filers.

What is the due date for GSTR-3B?

The 20th of the next month for monthly filers. Quarterly filers file by the 22nd or 24th of the month after the quarter, depending on their state.

Do I have to file if I had no sales?

Yes. A Nil return must still be filed on time, or a late fee of ₹20 per day applies. GrowwBizz files Nil returns for ₹200.

Who can file quarterly?

Businesses with aggregate turnover up to ₹5 crore can opt for the QRMP scheme. They file returns quarterly but pay tax monthly by the 25th using PMT-06.

What is the late fee for GSTR-3B?

₹50 per day (₹20 for Nil returns), capped by turnover, plus 18% yearly interest on any tax paid late.
