GrowwBizz Desk

Internal app for GrowwBizz staff: Orders Desk and Refer & Earn. Not for clients.

Download the app (APK)
  1. Open this page in Chrome on your Android phone and tap Download.
  2. If asked, allow Chrome to install apps: tap Settings, then turn on Allow from this source.
  3. Open the downloaded file and tap Install. If a "Play Protect" warning appears, tap Install anyway (the app is private, not on the Play Store).
  4. Open GrowwBizz Desk and log in with your own login.

Do not share this link outside the team.
