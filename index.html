Why make your DSC with us?Registration needs several online forms signed with the DSC.
  • With us: all forms get signed smoothly, with no back-and-forth.
  • Your own DSC: we must contact you again and again to sign each form, which can delay registration.
What is a DSC and do I need one?

A DSC (Digital Signature Certificate) is an online signature on a USB token. Every director or partner signs the government (MCA) forms with it, so each person needs one.

  • Best choice: make it with us at the time of registration. We handle the signing of all incorporation forms smoothly, without chasing you for each form.
  • Already have one? You can use it if it is a Class 3 DSC that is still valid (a DSC lasts 2 years), but expect us to ask you to sign several forms one by one.
